# Safety-training one-session-one-file mode

## Required evidence map

Use the uploaded template as authoritative. When no complete template exists, organize supported material in this order:

1. Cover: archive title, period, department or team.
2. Training plan: topic, instructor, date, hours, audience, training method, and approval fields.
3. Training handouts: one section per scheduled topic with matching date, instructor, and audience.
4. Attendance or sign-in evidence.
5. Examination paper, answer key, scores, or assessment evidence when supplied.
6. Training photographs or supporting records when supplied.
7. Comprehensive evaluation: factual summary, supported assessment, problems, improvement actions, instructors, and responsible person.
8. Other required registers or operating-process attachments only when the template or task explicitly includes them.

## Reconciliation rules

- Build a canonical session table before editing: topic, date, instructor, duration, audience, evidence files, attendance count, exam count, and result status.
- The plan, handout headers, attendance, assessment, and evaluation must agree with that table.
- Treat a missing signature or approval as missing evidence, not permission to type a person's name into a signature field.
- A narrative handout is not proof of attendance or examination.
- A statement such as “all passed” requires a complete score list or other explicit result evidence.
- Keep unrelated registers out unless the existing template requires them; record questionable inclusions for confirmation.

## Layout and output

- Preserve the supplied cover, table order, headers, signature areas, page breaks, and print layout.
- Keep photographs proportionate and captioned with supported date/location information only.
- Produce one editable DOCX archive plus an XLSX checklist; do not flatten the archive to images.
- The checklist is a Chinese business deliverable. Its first row must be exactly `环节、必备项目、依据来源、状态、冲突、备注`; do not expose internal English field names.
